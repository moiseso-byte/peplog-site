PepLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

PepLog is built to keep your information private by default. Everything you enter stays on your device. We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and it is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. PepLog has no account, no login, and no server. The app does not transmit your data off your device. We, the developer, receive no personal information from your use of the app.

What stays on your device

The information you choose to enter — such as your name, weight entries, the compounds you log, vial details, doses, injection sites, and notes — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. It is under your control. Deleting the app removes this data.

No third parties

PepLog contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or software development kits. Your health-related information is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties, because it never leaves your device.

No cloud storage

PepLog does not store your personal or health information in iCloud or any other cloud service.

Device backups

If you use Apple's standard device backup (iCloud Backup or an encrypted local backup), your PepLog data may be included in that backup, the same as other apps on your device. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own device settings, not by us.

Notifications

PepLog can show you local reminders and encouragement — for example, hydration check-ins or reminders you set for your own logging schedule. These notifications are created and scheduled entirely on your device: PepLog does not use push notifications, no notification content or data is sent to us or anyone else, and nothing about them leaves your device. Notifications are optional — you can decline the permission when asked, or turn them off anytime in iOS Settings.

Children

PepLog is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Medical disclaimer

PepLog is a personal logging and measurement tool. It does not provide medical advice, dosing recommendations, or treatment guidance, and it does not recommend or endorse any substance. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting, stopping, or changing any regimen.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Contact@builtfaster.org.

PepLog is an independent application. This policy applies only to the PepLog iOS app.

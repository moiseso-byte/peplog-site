Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. PepLog has no account, no cloud, and no analytics — nothing you enter is uploaded or shared.

How do I back it up?
Your PepLog data is included in your standard encrypted device backup, alongside your other apps.

Does PepLog recommend doses?
No. PepLog is a logging and measurement tool. It doesn't provide medical advice or dosing recommendations, and it doesn't recommend or endorse any substance. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before starting, stopping, or changing any regimen. For adults 18+.
